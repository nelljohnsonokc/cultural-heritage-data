A special heritage object: The death masks of Joseph & Hyrum Smith

Sacred to members of the Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-Day Saints, the death masks of prophet Joseph Smith and his brother Hyrum were placed on display at the Church History Museum in Salt Lake City, Utah in 1990. Before this, Smith's friend Philo Dibble exhibited the masks throughout Utah Territory, where they circulated as accessible points of heritage and faith for pioneering Saints. The Beehive State's arid atmosphere left the masks remarkably preserved. In the digital age, the Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-Day Saints has digitized the masks into various 3D formats available for public download.
